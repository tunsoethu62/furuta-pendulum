## Confirmed software defect and correction (v2.1)

The absolute-peak tracker was initialized with the preceding tip's magnitude and kept taking an absolute maximum. On an asymmetric +150 -> -120 degree half-swing, it reported 150 degrees again: energy 1.866 rather than the current tip's 1.500. This was reproduced by a failing regression against the actual detector. The log has asymmetric tips (for example -163.63 followed by about +136.96 degrees), consistent with that defect affecting pumping. Its exact contribution to the hardware shortfall is not separately measurable from this log.

V2.1 tracks a **signed directional extreme**. Positive travel tracks max(alpha); negative travel tracks min(alpha). Each tip now uses its own angle. This prevents a larger previous swing from masking a smaller one and reducing its next planned push.

A second issue appeared during verification: full rotations bypassed the turning-point loss update and retained excessive compensation, creating repeated simulated overshoots. A detected full rotation now halves the learned compensation before the next pump. The overshoot backoff is a bounded tuning heuristic; it does not prove hardware convergence.

No changes to A, capture gates, acceleration/speed caps, travel limits, countdown, timeout or stop behavior. New boot banner: `swingup-v2.1`.
